Hello everyone, it is 6:23 AM 10/12/2026. Yesterday at this time I was barely going to bed. Today I am up and fully rested at this same time.

I am trying to fix my sleep schedule. To be completely honest I haven't been trying very hard but today is a step in the right direction. Lately, this sense of needing to become a pilot has come back stronger than ever. I feel a major need to become something respectable in my life. Always have.

Although being a good person is in itself respectable and there is nothing wrong with just being another face in the crowd, I long for a meaning. A purpose that can solidify the toil I have put myself through not just since grade school has ended but even during it. People speak about how life is defined by moments and if you decide incorrectly you can set yourself back years without realizing it and I can attest to that but these moments do not define you. The biggest thing is how you react and adjust, you need to be flexible and find the good in every situation.

We are all we got. You can have help and you can help others. But no one is coming to save you from yourself. You have to make those decisions.

Get out and do the hard stuff, you'll be glad you did.

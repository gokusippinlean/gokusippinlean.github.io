I got a job offer from JPMorgan Chase yesterday November 5th 2026 at 3:03 PM. I didn't see it until about 3:30 or so. I was in the John Adams Elementary library. The students were rehearsing for The Jungle Book musical performance they are practicing for.

The room felt like it was spinning and my head was ready to explode out of pure emotions. The feeling of excitement, disbelief and gratitude was incredible. In my hands was a golden ticket into a new career that I never could've imagined. The world of banking, and not just any bank but Chase. This was monumental for me.

There was a wave of melancholy that washed over me soon after hearing the students sing. The rapport built with not only my one on one assignment but others in his classroom would soon be gone. There is a sense within me that I am abandoning these students. Honestly, they don't have many if any male figures in the school and the few that are there are rather effeminate to say the least.

Lot of the leads there see a young boys energy as problematic or a burden on them. A kid who wants to play basketball or soccer all day is look at as incapable of sitting still instead of seeing a kid with massive drive to do better in said activity. Even if just for a short stretch I am glad I was able to help them get more time outside since many teachers or aides see it as burdensome.

Back to the job offer, I filled out the most extensive background check I have ever filled out in my life. I am nervous, what if the chance to work for JPMorgan slips right by me. I have bounced between so many jobs these past 5 years and they wanted to know about every single one. I am fearful that something in my past comes up and prevents me from moving forward.

I filled out all my paperwork, completed all my onboarding tasks and ran my fingerprints earlier today. Now I just wait. They say no news is good news. Until someone says otherwise this reamins true.

In lighter news, should I get a charcoal suit? Feel like I need a charcoal suit. Really should stop racking up debt though. Still though if I end up leaving J. Crew I should leave with that before I go I think. 

Love you all. Cheers.

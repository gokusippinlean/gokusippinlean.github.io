Hi, my name is Anthony Giovannic Ponce and I am depressed. 

I have felt like this for a very long time and I don't see where it can be better. I feel disrespected and unappreciated by every person I've ever worked for and I hate where I am career wise.

I think I do a good job helping people. It is my job to make people feel good about the clothes they buy. It's also my job to help kids with disabilites feel like they have an opportunity same as everyone else. 

That feels rewarding. To know I am making a difference. I sold 5 thousand dollar of clothes and still feel like I didn't do anything meaningful.

This Tuesday at 10AM I will be having an interview with JPMorgan Chase for an associate banker role. Something tells me I won't get it. I am ready for that. 

To feel disappointed and passed over again. It's what life has always been for me. 

I try to not let the highs make me feel so high. That way the lows don't feel so low.

I should go for a run or something. I miss having that surge of dopamine from long runs. Thing is I hate what that's become. 

A bunch of self important dickheads who do it for kudos on Strava with 300 dollar super shoes as if they are puting down anything more than 8 and half minute miles. Maybe that's why I couldn't cut it at Hoka. 

Bunch of marks.

The older you get the more you find comfort in nostalgia I suppose. I can't even enjoy that anymore. It is just a bitter reminder of how much time passed and how the world has changed.

As of late I've slacked off entirely on my schooling. This is something that only furthers how terrible I feel.

I want to thank Tarieq for going on vacation with me to Thailand. That was a lot of fun and we both seem to miss it dearly. We want to go back in April. Hope that comes to fruition.

Maybe life really is just seeing pants go from wide to slim over and over until you die.

Do you still think about me sometimes? I think about us a lot. It's been 5 years and not a day goes by I don't think about it.

It can never be put back together. Better to have loved and lost than never loved at all right? I'm not so sure anymore. This pain won't go away.

The thoughts of suicide idealation have come back very strong. There is a part of me that wants to have someone else hold them away from me but I know that I could follow through anyway. There is always my dreams I can look forward to where sometimes just for a little we are together again. Until my conscious takes away my peace and jolts me into this life I've made for myself.

I don't think I can fall in love again. It was too perfect.

Sincerely yours,

Anthony Giovannic Ponce
